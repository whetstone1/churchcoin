# churchcoin

ChurchCoin is a blockchain-based stablecoin designed to track and reflect the stability of religious institutions, starting with the Catholic Church. By leveraging a custom stability index composed of key performance indicators such as financial health, community engagement, and public sentiment, ChurchCoin offers a unique digital asset that embodies the resilience and influence of these institutions.

The coin integrates real-world data through oracles, adjusting its value to provide a dynamic and stable digital currency. ChurchCoin aims to serve as a financial tool for religious communities, enabling secure donations, supporting church missions, and fostering a new level of financial transparency and engagement within the religious sector.
